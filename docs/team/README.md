# Component Warehouse · Team

Team mode is used to share component libraries, PCB records, projects, purchases, risks, members, and operation logs.

Path: `/component-warehouse/team/`

## Team Components

Team components can bind to a member's personal inventory item. While the source member remains in the team, inventory quantities are read live from personal inventory.

## Roles

- Captain: manage the team, members, rebinding, and key operations.
- Editor: maintain team components and projects.
- Reader: view data only.

## Labels and Scan Pages

Team and personal labels share public component scan URLs. Public pages show a safe summary only; signed-in users with permission can open management views.

## Projects

Team projects support BOM import, shortage review, soldering progress, purchases, and risk tracking.
